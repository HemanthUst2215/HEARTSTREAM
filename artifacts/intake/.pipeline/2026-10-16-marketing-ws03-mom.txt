1 SUMMARY 
Meeting: Customer Insights (Marketing) Workshop 
Date: 30th September 2026 Time: 06.00 AM – 09.00 AM PST 
Meeting 
Facilitator: 
UST Team Place: Microsoft Teams 
Meeting 
Objective: 
1) Review Dynamics 365 Customer Insights (Marketing) capabilities and applicability for 
HeartStream. 
2) Understand current marketing processes, customer insight sources, and campaign 
management practices. 
3) Identify marketing data sources, segmentation requirements, and customer/distributor 
profiling needs. 
4) Discuss content governance, asset management, approval processes, and campaign 
readiness requirements. 
5) Understand reporting, KPI, consent management, and marketing performance 
measurement needs. 
6) Capture requirements, gaps, assumptions, and follow-up actions for future-state 
marketing capabilities. 
HeartStream Participants: James Casella, Brian Arbanas, Mattias Jarnstrom, Kaitie Carrigan, Rajeev 
Dhorajiwala and other business representatives 
UST Participants: CRM, Marketing, Integration, Data, Architecture, QA and Program representatives 
 
 
            
• HeartStream acknowledged the potential value of leveraging 
unified customer and partner data to support targeted marketing 
initiatives. 
 
• It was agreed that Customer Insights could act as a key enabler 
for future segmentation, campaign management and marketing 
analytics requirements. 
 
3 Customer and partner 
profile requirements 
• HeartStream requested that the solution provide a consolidated 
account-level view while allowing users to drill down into 
associated contacts when required. 
 
• It was agreed that marketing teams should be able to analyse 
customers, distributors, hospitals and partner organizations 
within a unified view. 
 
• HeartStream emphasized that global distributors operating 
across multiple regions must be supported within the future-state 
customer and partner profile model. 
 
• HeartStream confirmed that future marketing activities must 
support both distributor-led and direct-to-customer business 
models. 
 
4 Segmentation strategy and 
market visibility 
• HeartStream highlighted the need to segment customers by key 
market categories such as Hospitals, EMS, Fire, Military and AED 
Channels 
 
• It was requested that future segmentation capabilities also 
support more granular verticals such as Dental, Schools, 
Universities, Airports and other end-customer markets. 
 
• HeartStream acknowledged that limited visibility currently exists 
into where distributors ultimately sell products, restricting 
effective market segmentation and analysis. 
 
• It was agreed that additional end-customer data capture and 
device registration processes may be required to improve future 
segmentation accuracy. 
 
5 Marketing insight and 
research sources 
• HeartStream confirmed that traditional market research reports 
have provided limited value and are not currently a primary 
source of marketing intelligence 
 
• HeartStream indicated that customer surveys have not been 
consistently conducted in recent years but would be valuable to 
reintroduce as part of future marketing initiatives. 
 
• It was highlighted that distributor feedback, sales team input and 
direct market conversations currently serve as the primary 
sources of marketing insight. 
 
 
            
 
• HeartStream confirmed that industry market-share information is 
received periodically through third-party consolidated reports and 
could potentially be leveraged for analysis. 
 
6 Customer feedback and 
survey management 
• HeartStream expressed interest in implementing structured 
customer satisfaction surveys as part of future-state marketing 
capabilities. 
 
• It was suggested that direct customers could receive surveys 
following product purchase, onboarding or product usage 
milestones. 
 
• HeartStream indicated that customer feedback, complaints and 
product experience data should be available for future marketing 
and product planning activities. 
 
• UST highlighted that Customer Insights can utilize feedback-
related information as an additional source for segmentation and 
analysis. 
 
7 Marketing data sources 
and profile enrichment 
• HeartStream confirmed that marketing insights may originate 
from CRM, eCommerce, trade shows, customer feedback 
systems, sales transactions and distributor-provided information. 
 
• It was discussed that future-state profiling should combine 
multiple sources to create richer customer and partner insights. 
 
• HeartStream emphasized the importance of capturing meaningful 
customer information as early as possible in the customer 
lifecycle. 
 
• It was agreed that improved data capture processes would 
directly impact the quality of segmentation and marketing 
outcomes. 
 
8 Customer master data and 
migration considerations 
• HeartStream highlighted concerns regarding duplicate customer 
records, fragmented account structures and inconsistent 
customer numbering across existing systems. 
 
• It was agreed that customer data cleansing will be critical prior to 
migration into Dynamics 365. 
 
• HeartStream emphasized that poor-quality migrated data could 
significantly reduce the value of marketing insights and 
segmentation capabilities. 
 
• UST confirmed that data quality, deduplication and account 
rationalization activities will be important considerations during 
migration planning. 
 
 
 
            
9 Market segmentation data 
capture 
• HeartStream stressed the importance of capturing market 
segmentation information at the earliest possible stage of the 
sales process 
 
• It was suggested that sales, quoting and order processes should 
capture market classifications wherever feasible. 
 
• HeartStream noted that market segmentation captured at order 
entry would provide significantly better marketing intelligence 
than attempting classification months after product shipment 
 
• It was agreed that future-state processes should support 
improved segmentation data collection across the commercial 
lifecycle 
 
10 Marketing content 
governance 
• HeartStream confirmed that externally published marketing 
content currently undergoes approval by Marketing, Quality, 
Clinical, Legal and Regulatory stakeholders. 
 
• It was emphasized that only approved content should be used in 
customer-facing communications and campaigns. 
 
• HeartStream differentiated between formally approved customer-
facing content and internal competitive intelligence used for 
business planning purposes. 
 
• It was suggested that future solutions should support appropriate 
controls around approved and non-approved content usage. 
 
11 MediaValet content 
management approach 
• HeartStream confirmed that MediaValet currently serves as the 
central repository for approved marketing collateral, images, 
brochures and related assets. 
 
• It was discussed that MediaValet is primarily used for storage, 
retrieval and sharing of approved content with internal teams and 
channel partners. 
 
• HeartStream requested an assessment of whether Dynamics 365 
could complement or replace portions of the current MediaValet 
functionality. 
 
• It was agreed that further evaluation is required before any future-
state decisions are made regarding content management 
architecture. 
 
12 Marketing campaign 
execution 
• HeartStream expressed strong interest in leveraging automated 
marketing journeys based on customer behavior, purchase 
history and segmentation criteria. 
 
• It was demonstrated that campaigns could be triggered through 
predefined segments or business events within Dynamics 365. 
 
 
            
 
• HeartStream acknowledged the value of automated customer 
outreach for activities such as product awareness, upgrade 
campaigns and engagement programs. 
 
• It was agreed that future campaign capabilities should support 
distributor-focused as well as direct-customer marketing 
initiatives. 
 
13 Campaign approvals and 
governance 
• HeartStream clarified that content approvals and campaign 
approvals are separate processes and should be treated 
independently. 
 
• It was agreed that previously approved content should not require 
full regulatory review each time it is reused within a campaign. 
 
• HeartStream indicated that campaign execution should still be 
subject to appropriate marketing review and approval controls. 
 
• It was noted that campaigns involving promotions, discounts or 
commercial incentives may require additional finance approval 
before launch. 
 
14 
Marketing performance 
measurement and 
reporting 
• HeartStream indicated that current campaign reporting 
capabilities are limited and require improvement in the future-
state solution. 
 
• It was agreed that marketing performance should be measurable 
through engagement metrics, landing page activity, click-through 
rates and conversion indicators 
 
• HeartStream highlighted the need to understand campaign 
effectiveness and its contribution to downstream sales 
outcomes. 
 
• It was discussed that reporting should provide visibility into 
customer and segment-level trends to support future marketing 
decisions. 
 
15 Marketing channels and 
consent management 
• HeartStream confirmed that email is expected to be the primary 
marketing channel during initial rollout phases. 
 
• HeartStream expressed interest in supporting additional channels 
such as SMS, social media and other digital engagement methods 
in the future. 
 
• It was agreed that opt-in, opt-out and communication preference 
management capabilities are required for future marketing 
activities. 
 
 
 
            
• HeartStream confirmed that future marketing processes must 
comply with applicable privacy and consent management 
requirements, including GDPR obligations where relevant. 
 
16 Marketing organization and 
access requirements 
• HeartStream confirmed that the marketing organization operates 
with a relatively small global team compared to the legacy Philips 
model. 
 
• It was agreed that marketing users require broad visibility into 
customers, install base, sales activity and related business 
information to support planning and segmentation activities. 
 
• HeartStream indicated that only a limited number of users are 
expected to create and manage campaigns globally. 
 
• It was confirmed that appropriate role-based access controls will 
be required while still enabling global visibility for authorized 
marketing users. 
 
 
 
3 DECISIONS MADE 
Sr Topic Discussion Summary 
1 Marketing stakeholder 
participation 
• It was agreed that future Marketing / Customer Insights 
workshops should include additional marketing stakeholders, as 
the current workshop attendance was impacted by confusion 
around the workshop title and scope. 
 
2 Customer profile approach 
• It was agreed that the future-state marketing solution should 
primarily support account-level visibility while allowing users to 
drill down into associated contacts when required. 
 
3 Future-state segmentation 
strategy 
• It was agreed that customer and distributor segmentation will be 
a key capability of the future solution. 
 
• Initial focus should be on major market segments such as 
Hospital, EMS, Fire, Military and AED Channels, with the ability to 
expand into more granular vertical markets over time. 
 
4 Customer Insights data 
model 
• It was agreed that customer, distributor, partner and direct-
consumer information should be considered when defining future 
Customer Insights profiles and segmentation strategies. 
 
5 Customer survey capability 
• HeartStream agreed that customer surveys and feedback 
collection should form part of the future-state marketing 
capability to improve customer understanding and insight 
generation. 
 
6 Marketing content 
governance 
• It was agreed that only approved marketing content should be 
used in marketing campaigns and customer communications. 
 
 
            
 
• Existing Legal, Regulatory, Clinical, Quality and Marketing 
approval processes will continue to govern content approval. 
 
7 Campaign approval model 
• It was agreed that content approval and campaign approval are 
separate processes. 
 
• Approved content may be reused in campaigns without repeating 
full content approval activities, while campaign execution will 
remain subject to appropriate business approvals. 
 
8 Marketing data visibility 
• HeartStream confirmed that authorized marketing users should 
have visibility to customer, install base, sales and related 
business information required for segmentation, planning and 
campaign execution. 
 
9 Consent management 
approach 
• It was agreed that future marketing campaigns must support 
consent management, opt-in/opt-out capabilities and applicable 
GDPR/privacy compliance requirements. 
 
10 Marketing channels 
roadmap 
• HeartStream confirmed email as the primary initial marketing 
channel. 
 
• SMS, social media and additional digital engagement channels 
will be considered as future-state capabilities. 
 
11 Customer data quality and 
migration 
• It was agreed that customer master data cleansing, deduplication 
and account rationalization activities will be required prior to 
migration to ensure effective marketing segmentation and 
reporting. 
 
12 Segmentation data capture 
• It was agreed that market segmentation information should be 
captured as early as possible within the commercial process to 
support future marketing analytics and reporting needs.
